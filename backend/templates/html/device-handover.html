5. Terms of use

  • Company use only. The device is provided for company work. It stays signed in to the company accounts listed above.

If the device is lost while with the employee, or not returned when they leave, its recovery value ({recoveryValue}) and the value of any missing accessory may be recovered, including from the full & final settlement, subject to applicable law.
